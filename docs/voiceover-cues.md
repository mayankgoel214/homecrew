## How to record
- One voice memo per line, named exactly `vo1` … `vo7`. Any phone format is fine (.m4a).
- Quiet room, phone about 6 inches from your mouth, normal speaking voice, steady pace.
- Leave about a second of silence before you start (I trim it). Do 2–3 takes and send the best.
- Read it out loud with a timer once first. If it runs over the time, talk a little faster, don't cut words.
